What WhatRock is

WhatRock identifies rocks, minerals and fossils from a photo and shows an educational best guess with a confidence level and field notes. It is a learning and field-reference tool for your own curiosity, not a laboratory analysis, an appraisal, or a certification of any kind.

Identifications are educational, not laboratory results

WhatRock does not appraise, certify or authenticate anything. Every identification is a best-effort guess from an automated vision model, shown with a confidence level (High, Medium or Low) so you know how much to trust it. A low-confidence result shows up to three possible candidates instead of a single confident-sounding guess it is not actually sure of.

Accuracy depends on your photo — lighting, focus, and how much of the specimen is visible — and on the specimen itself; some material genuinely cannot be told apart from a photo alone. Gem-grade material (for example diamond, ruby, sapphire, emerald, gold or meteorite) is always shown at Low confidence with a caution, because only a qualified gem lab or geologist can confirm those. WhatRock is not a substitute for a professional geologist, gemologist or accredited testing lab, and nothing in the app is a guarantee of identity, authenticity or composition.

Collecting responsibly

WhatRock does not encourage or endorse collecting anywhere it is prohibited. You are solely responsible for knowing and following the law and the land rules that apply to you — for example, collecting rocks, minerals and fossils is prohibited in United States national parks and restricted in many other public and private areas. Field tips shown in the app are general education, not a recommendation to collect on any specific site.

Safety

Any field test suggested in the app (for example a scratch test, a streak test, a magnet test, or a household-vinegar test for carbonates) is performed at your own risk. Never taste, lick or ingest a specimen. Never use acids other than what is explicitly described, and never heat or forcibly break a specimen to test it. WhatRock is not liable for any injury, property damage, or loss arising from a field test or from collecting activity.
